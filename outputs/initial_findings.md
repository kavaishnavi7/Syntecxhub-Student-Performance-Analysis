## Data limitations
The supplied file has no attendance percentage, study hours, class/section, or dates. These requested factors and time trends need additional columns before they can be analyzed.

## Suggested actions
1. Review students with lower subject scores and offer targeted practice.
2. Compare score patterns across subjects to identify where extra learning resources may be useful.
3. Collect attendance, study-hour, class/section, and repeated assessment data for future analysis.
4. Verify official grading thresholds before making student-level decisions.
